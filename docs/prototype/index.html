Our framework

A transaction discipline built for a high-scrutiny market.

West African gold carries a reputation that serious buyers cannot ignore. Our framework is designed to answer every question a refinery compliance officer will ask — before the first introduction is made.

Compliance Before Introduction

No seller documentation is released until a counterparty has completed KYC, submitted a Client Information Sheet and provided verifiable proof of funds.

Non-Custodial by Design

Orvex never takes title to metal and never holds buyer funds. Payment flows directly between the buyer's bank and the authorized exporter's bank.

Global Standards, Local Execution

Our process is aligned with OECD due diligence guidance and Burkina Faso's Mining Code, and executed on the ground in Ouagadougou by vetted, licensed partners.

Verification at Every Stage

Export authorizations, permits and assay results are independently verified before settlement, not after.

Standing commitments

Commitments we hold on every engagement.

Zero upfront fees

Buyers are never asked to pay taxes, customs or handling fees before a verified assay.

Bank-to-bank settlement

Funds move only between the buyer's bank and the licensed exporter's bank.

NCNDA & IMFPA on every deal

Every introduction is protected by executed non-circumvention and fee-protection agreements.

OECD-aligned due diligence

Supply-chain risk is assessed against the OECD framework for high-risk areas.

Bilingual documentation

Every engagement is supported in English and French, the working language in Burkina Faso.
